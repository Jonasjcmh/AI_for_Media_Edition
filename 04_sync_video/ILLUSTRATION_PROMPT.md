# Illustration of the experimental setup (optional)

`sync_diagram.png` in this folder is the diagram used in the slides (made in code,
labels match the real numbers). If you also want a picture of the **physical setup**,
paste the prompt below into ChatGPT (image generation), then save the result here
as `setup_illustration.png`.

Label any generated image **"AI-generated illustration"** wherever you use it: it
shows the idea, not your real lab.

---

**Prompt (copy everything below):**

> Create a clean, flat-vector technical illustration of a lab experiment, 16:9,
> white background, warm brown and orange palette (#7A5C3E, #C0703A, #E0904A) with
> dark grey (#2E3138), no gradients, no photorealism, no text except the labels listed.
>
> Scene, left to right:
> 1. A **video camera on a tripod** pointing at the experiment, with a dotted
>    field-of-view cone.
> 2. In the middle, a **steel ball (orange)** shown at three heights with faint motion
>    arcs, bouncing on a **flat force plate** (dark grey slab) on a table. Small dashed
>    arc lines show the bounces getting lower.
> 3. Next to the plate, a **small LED** on a post, glowing, clearly inside the camera's
>    field of view.
> 4. On the right, a **data logger box** with a cable to the force plate and a second
>    cable to the LED, and a small screen showing a step signal and spikes.
> 5. Below everything, **two horizontal timelines**: the top one a strip of film frames
>    labelled "Camera 30 fps", the bottom one a fine waveform labelled "Logger 1 kHz".
>    A vertical dashed line joins one highlighted film frame (LED lit) to the step in the
>    waveform, labelled "sync event".
>
> Labels only: "Camera", "Force plate", "LED", "Data logger", "Camera 30 fps",
> "Logger 1 kHz", "sync event". Simple sans-serif font, generous whitespace, suitable
> for a scientific presentation slide.

**Check the result** before using it: the LED must be in the camera's view, the cables
must go to the logger, and the labels must be spelled correctly. Regenerate or edit if not.
